**Full description** (4000 max, 1,495 used):

```
Draw a loop, make it home, and the land inside is yours.

Territory Wars is a quick, colourful arcade game about claiming space. Leave your land to draw a trail, then curve back home to capture everything you circled. The bigger the loop, the bigger the reward, but your trail is your weak spot while you are out.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag anywhere to steer. One finger is all it takes.
• Leave your land to draw a trail, then return to claim the area inside.
• If a rival runs into your trail, it catches fire. Race home before the flames reach you!
• Run into a rival's trail to set it burning and knock them out.
• Knock out a rival and take their land for yourself.

FEATURES
• Fast rounds you can play anywhere, even offline.
• Three difficulties: Easy, Normal and Hard, with smarter and more numerous rival bots.
• A live leaderboard and minimap so you always know who is winning.
• One revive per round: watch a short ad to keep 100% of your land, with a 2-second shield.
• 11 skins with faces and moods that react to the game. Unlock them by playing and earning achievements, or with a short ad. No purchases.
• Personal bests for every difficulty and lifetime career stats.
• Satisfying sound effects and haptics, each with an on/off switch.
• Optional "pass through" mode so crossing your own trail is forgiven.

Can you claim the whole map? Loop out, come home, and find out.

Territory Wars is free to play and supported by ads. No account needed: your progress stays on your device.
```
