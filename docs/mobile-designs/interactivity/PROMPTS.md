## 1. Danger rising (far from home)
Territory Wars v2 — in-game moment "Danger rising (far from home)". Portrait mobile gameplay frame, top-down pastel arena (#F8F7F3 with faint grid), chunky pastel territories with darker lip edges. The player's periwinkle (#8DAAEC) rounded-cube head with a determined face is far outside its home territory, dragging a long translucent periwinkle trail that loops out from home. Minimal translucent HUD: top-left "12.4% · Kills 2", top-right compact 5-row leaderboard (player row bold), bottom-right circular minimap, bottom-left speaker button. Ink #34323F text only. No ads. Original art.

## 3. Threat warning
Territory Wars v2 — in-game moment "Threat warning". Portrait mobile gameplay frame, pastel top-down arena (#F8F7F3, faint grid) with chunky pastel territories that have darker lip edges. No ads. Original art.

## 5. Knockout freeze-frame (hit-stop)
Territory Wars v2 — in-game moment "Knockout freeze-frame". Portrait mobile gameplay frame, camera slightly zoomed in on the action. The player's periwinkle rounded cube (fierce happy face) has just knocked out a rival: the rival's sunny-yellow head bursts into chunky square confetti in its colour, frozen mid-air, with short white impact lines radiating from the hit point and a brief white flash ring. The rival's territory darkens to its lip shade and sinks. A streak banner slides in at top-center: gold #FFC93C pill with lip shadow, Rubik 900 ink text "KNOCKOUT!" plus a smaller "×3 STREAK" chip with a flame icon; a floating "+1" kill badge above the player. Subtle radial blur at the edges sells the freeze. Minimal HUD: top-left "15.0% · Kills 3" with the kills number bumped, top-right compact leaderboard, bottom-right minimap. Ink text only. No ads. Original art.

## 6. Touch steering feedback
Territory Wars v2 — in-game moment "Touch steering feedback". Portrait mobile gameplay frame, pastel top-down arena. In the lower half, a floating virtual joystick shown only while touching: a soft translucent white ring (~120px) with a faint lip shadow where the finger first touched, and a smaller filled periwinkle knob offset toward the drag direction with a subtle heading arrow. Ink text only. No ads. Original art.

## 8. Sound & Feel settings
Territory Wars v2 — "Sound & Feel" settings screen. Menu style: warm paper #E4E2DC background, white card radius 22px. Header with back chevron and title "Sound & Feel" (Rubik 900). Sections with uppercase Rubik 700 labels:
- AUDIO: "Music" slider with note icon (60%); "Sound effects" slider (80%); toggle "Directional sound" with helper "Hear fights off-screen from the side they happen".
- FEEL: toggle "Vibration" with a segmented control below (Off / Light / Strong); toggle "Danger warnings" with helper "Edge arrows and heartbeat when rivals near your trail"; toggle "Screen shake & freeze-frames".
- ACCESSIBILITY: toggle "Reduce motion" with helper "Softer animations, no flashes".

A chunky secondary "Test sounds" button with a speaker icon. Toggles are chunky pill switches in periwinkle #8DAAEC with lip; sliders have chunky round thumbs with lip shadow. Ink text only, body ≥16px. Optional anchored banner ad at the bottom labelled "Ad", ≥32px from controls. No prices or purchases. Original design.
