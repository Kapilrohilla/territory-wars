Territory Wars Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Developer: Kapil Rohilla

This policy explains what information the Territory Wars mobile game ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game itself keeps everything on your device, and the only third party involved is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

Information stored only on your device

The app saves your player name, best scores and records, unlocked skins, and settings (such as sound, vibration and difficulty) in your device's local storage so they are there next time you play. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

We do not ask for an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to revive, and an ad on the results screen. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android advertising IDAd serving, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention and, where you allow it, personalized ads
App interactions with ads (views, taps)Ad measurement and analytics
Approximate location derived from IP addressShowing ads relevant to your region and complying with local law
Diagnostics (crash and performance data from the ads SDK)Keeping ads working reliably

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy & ads option on the app's Records screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings.

Children

Territory Wars is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

We do not hold any of your data on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
